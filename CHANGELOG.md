# Changelog

## Unreleased
- PingID push step-up (mock) is now real on the server: `POST /api/mfa/push`, `GET /api/mfa/poll/:id` and `POST /api/mfa/push/:id/respond` (new `server/src/push.js`, tested), declared with `step_up_method: "pingid-push"`. The push dialog, which already existed on the page but had no server behind it, now works: it shows a labelled mock phone (Approve / "No, it's not me"), reports the push to the token reel, and polls without restarting on every re-render. Approval mints a single-use, amount-bound credit of its own kind (`push`), valid 5 minutes; a push that nobody answers fails after 60 s; a decided push cannot change; DENY still outranks it. New PUSH scenario, hops, reel cards, flow (drawn on the phone box), a fact (UNVERIFIED: an API-driven push was not measured, only the sign-in chain) and a talk-track entry. Nothing is sent to PingOne or a real phone.
- `npm run privilege-verify` (owner-run): one real held call against your Privilege gateway plus your approval in the console, then prints whether a held call answers 403 JSON-RPC or a 200 with pending_approval text, and whether the approval id is the instruction id or the one the console shows. Tested against stub gateways; never run by agents.
- Transfer gate tab reordered into four numbered steps, top to bottom: 1 Pick a scenario, 2 Watch the flow, 3 Token reel, 4 Gate result. "Try your own call / change the bands" and "Scenario (decoded) and what we know" are folded away. The five side-by-side columns are gone.
- Watch the flow: the lab's own diagram, drawn live from the run. Arrows walked so far are solid, the current one is bold, and the one that comes next while paused at a human beat is dimmed. A "Now" line says in words where the run is. An arrow that would run through another box goes round it. The mouse wheel scrolls the page over this diagram (zoom with the controls). The Diagram tab (Simulate, speed, Reset) is unchanged.
- Token reel: one numbered card per credential in the order it appears (agent bearer placeholder, consent challenge / MFA challenge / CIBA auth_req_id, the single-use amount-bound receipt or 5-minute MFA credit, the transfer), each labelled MOCK with a state (waiting, issued, consumed on retry, denied, refused) and the raw fields. The lab mints no agent JWT, and the bearer card says so. A DENY issues no credential.
- The human-beat dialogs (consent, MFA, CIBA) open at the right edge so they no longer cover the flow and the reel.
- Pure helpers in `web/src/liveFlow.js` (unit tested).
- New scenario "PRIVILEGE: $150 held for PingID approval" (mock): a Privilege-gateway "Require human approval" hold answers 403 `pending_approval` with an `ins_` instruction id; the approver decides it in a mock HITL Requests dialog; approving needs a mock PingID confirmation (push, or the demo passcode); the agent retries with `X-Procyon-Approval-Id` within 90 s and the transfer runs once (single transaction, amount-bound, 120 s to decide). Reject needs no PingID. New `server/src/privilege.js`, `GET /api/privilege/hitl`, `POST /api/privilege/hitl/:id/decide`, declared step-up `privilege` (amount-independent, still below a hard DENY), two diagram boxes (approver, PingID), four token-reel cards, three facts (two measured from the owner's 2026-11-17 recording, one UNVERIFIED for our own tenant) and a talk-track entry. 
- Real mode (owner-run): `HMLAB_REAL_URL` + `HMLAB_REAL_AGENT_KEY` + `HMLAB_REAL_TOOL` (+ optional `HMLAB_REAL_ARGS`) in the lab's own `.env` make the PRIVILEGE scenario send one real MCP `tools/call` to your Privilege gateway and show its real answer. New `server/src/privilegeReal.js` (handshake, bounded body, timeout, no redirect, key scrubbed, one call in flight), `GET /api/privilege/real`, `POST /api/privilege/real/call`, a runtime Mock/Real mode (`POST /api/mode` now switches it), and a netguard allowlist that opens exactly the one configured host while Real is on and closes it for Mock. The shared `gateway-trace` ("What Privilege did", rule 25) is vendored byte for byte with its pin and test. The reel marks Real cards REAL and the PingID step INFERRED (the lab never sees it); the banner says what is real. The old reserved `HMLAB_REAL_CLIENT_ID/SECRET` names are gone. Verified against a loopback stub gateway, not against a real tenant.
- Diagram: arrow labels are drawn in a layer above the boxes and placed so none overlaps another label or sits on a box (the walked arrow's label turns bold and coloured and is raised); click a box and it expands in place with its details below its title: its kind, reach (boxes upstream and downstream), and the arrows in and out (each opens that box), on a card with colour that means something (blue where it is, green what it sends to, amber what reaches it). Click again, Close, the empty canvas or Reset collapses it; Copy link and `?box=<id>` open the page with a box expanded. The shared `diagram-kit` (vendored into `web/src/diagram-kit/`, pinned) does the work.

- Verification mark in the header: the shared `VerifyMark` (vendored from AI-DEMO-Standalone `shared/verify-mark`, byte for byte, pinned) shows this lab's Verified / In progress mark from the demo shelf, inside the top menu beside the Shelf link.
